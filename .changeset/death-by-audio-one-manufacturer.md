Death By Audio is one manufacturer again.

Its 30 products sat under a second manufacturer file, `deathbyaudio`, beside the `death-by-audio` one the tier list imports against, so an import could not see them and duplicated them (catalog#958, closed). The entries move to `death-by-audio` with their ids unchanged, the manufacturer keeps the `death-by-audio` id, and the `deathbyaudio` id is retired.
